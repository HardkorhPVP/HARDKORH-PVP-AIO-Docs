HARDKORH grows one class pack at a time on the same [platform]({{ '/hardkorh/platform/' | relative_url }}): every new specialization inherits the shared targeting, strategy, control, defensive, interrupt and display intelligence, and adds the knowledge of its own class.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="hk-pill hk-pill--live">DONE</span> | Complete and documented on this site. |
| <span class="hk-pill hk-pill--planned">PLANNED</span> | On the roadmap, not yet available. |

Classes are listed in roadmap order. No release dates are announced. HARDKORH builds damage and healing specializations only; tank specializations are not on the roadmap.

## Class packs

Each class becomes one class pack with all of its HARDKORH specializations inside, and **HARDKORH PVP AIO** brings together every available class pack. Pricing and availability of individual class packs will be announced when they are finalized. See [the HARDKORH platform]({{ '/hardkorh/platform/' | relative_url }}).

Questions and suggestions are welcome on [Discord](https://discord.gg/sv2WNwk2WZ).
