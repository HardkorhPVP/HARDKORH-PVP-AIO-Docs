## 1. Open HARDKORH PVP AIO

Log in on your Feral Druid and open the Project Sylvanas menu, then open **HARDKORH PVP AIO**. Every setting lives on this one page as a stack of section cards: GENERAL OPTIONS, KORHVISION, KORHSIGHT, KORHTRAP, BURST, KORHSHIELD, KORHKICK, LOGGING & SUPPORT and ADVANCED, with SYSTEM STATUS and SUPPORT DIAGNOSTICS at the bottom. Just scroll down; there are no internal pages to switch between.

## 2. Check the state

On a new install the Combat HUD on screen reads <span class="hk-pill hk-pill--disabled">DISABLED</span>, and SYSTEM STATUS at the bottom of the page reads HARDKORH: DISABLED - no automation.

## 3. Assign your keybinds

Optional, recommended. All four player keybinds are unbound on a new install, and you choose your own keys: click a keybind field on the HARDKORH page, then press the key you want. See [Keybinds]({{ '/getting-started/keybinds/' | relative_url }}).

## 4. Place the HUD and review the settings

Drag the HUD window where you want it. It remembers the spot (HUD Left and HUD Top in the KORHVISION section follow the drag), and HUD Scale picks the text size. Then review the [settings]({{ '/hardkorh/settings/' | relative_url }}); the recommended values are listed there.

## 5. Enable the plugin

Turn on **Enable Plugin** with its switch or its key. The HUD badge reads <span class="hk-pill hk-pill--live">LIVE</span> and SYSTEM STATUS reads HARDKORH: LIVE. No separate arm step exists.

## 6. Turn on this session's LIVE controls

<div class="notice--warning" markdown="1">
**After every reload: session controls**

These controls are session-only. They return to OFF or SHADOW on every reload or relog, so set them each session before you queue:

- **Option C Live Targeting**: turn ON for PvP.
- **CC Healer**: turn ON.
- **Incapacitating Roar Live**: turn ON.
- **Cyclone Live**: turn ON.
- **Arena Burst**: turn ON with the switch or the Arena Burst Key.
- **Strength of the Wild Live**: turn ON.

Enable Plugin, the HUD settings, the keybinds and every other checkbox and slider are saved between sessions.
</div>

## 7. Read KORHVISION

The HUD shows what HARDKORH sees and does: the KILL target, the CONTROL target, the current action, burst, defense, Bear and CC state. Learn the rows on the [KORHVISION]({{ '/hardkorh/korhvision/' | relative_url }}) page.
