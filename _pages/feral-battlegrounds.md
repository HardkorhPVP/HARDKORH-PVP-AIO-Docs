## Target selection

With Option C LIVE, HARDKORH keeps your chosen enemy player as the KILL. Pick targets deliberately, such as a healer you can reach or a low-health player. When you have no target, the Project Sylvanas target selector's player can be used.

## Team-fight pressure

In Battlegrounds HARDKORH bursts in Cat with Rake and Rip up on the target, and lines the major cooldown up with Tiger's Fury when Tiger's Fury is ready. It holds burst into immunities, Cyclone, breakable CC and heavy damage reduction.

## Objectives are yours

<div class="notice--info" markdown="1">
HARDKORH has no knowledge of flags, bases or objectives. Where to fight and when to rotate is entirely your call.
</div>

## Crowd control

With CC Healer ON, Roar and Cyclone look for the nearest validated enemy healer within range. In large fights, position near the enemy healer to give KORHTRAP its window. See [KORHTRAP]({{ '/hardkorh/korhtrap/' | relative_url }}).

## Defensive survival

The same layered defensives as in Arena apply, including the defensive Bear Form under pressure. See [KORHSHIELD]({{ '/hardkorh/korhshield/' | relative_url }}).

## Mobility and utility

Wild Charge closes the gap to the KILL. Dash, Stampeding Roar and Travel Form stay manual.

## Using KORHVISION

Glance at KILL and HOLD to confirm what you are attacking and why damage stopped. Glance at BURST to see if your cooldowns are about to fire. See [KORHVISION]({{ '/hardkorh/korhvision/' | relative_url }}).
