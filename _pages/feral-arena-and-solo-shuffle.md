HARDKORH is strongest when you let it do what it is good at, and you do what only you can do.

<div class="notice--success" markdown="1">

Enable Plugin ON (HUD LIVE), Option C LIVE, CC Healer ON, Roar Live ON, Cyclone Live ON, Arena Burst ON. Mark of the Wild is refreshed automatically out of combat when it is missing.

## Who does what

| HARDKORH handles | You handle |
| --- | --- |
| The Cat rotation and bleed upkeep | **All movement:** HARDKORH never moves you |
| Holding one KILL target (Option C LIVE) | **All positioning and facing:** line of sight, pillars, range |
| Incapacitating Roar and Cyclone timing | **Target choice:** your target switch drives the KILL |
| Burst timing per environment | **Strategy:** when to go in, when to reset, which fight to take |
| Barkskin, Survival Instincts, Healthstone, Regrowth procs | Abilities HARDKORH does not cast: Dash, Stampeding Roar, Prowl, Travel Form, Ursol's Vortex, Entangling Roots, Maim, trinkets |
| Defensive Bear Form and the return to Cat | Standing still when the Cyclone cue says so |
| Skull Bash interrupts; Wild Charge, Typhoon, Heart of the Wild, Mark of the Wild | |

## Opening

From stealth, HARDKORH opens with Rake and builds its bleeds. Target the enemy you want to kill; with Option C LIVE that player becomes the KILL and stays the KILL. Get into melee range: Wild Charge closes the gap toward a KILL outside melee range when it is castable.

## Pressure and the KILL target

- The KILL row shows exactly who is taking your damage.
- The KILL does not flicker to whoever is closest; it stays until you switch target, the player dies, or it is lost for more than a moment.
- To switch pressure, simply target the new enemy player. Your switch always wins.

KILL is your damage target; CONTROL is the CC target, with CC Healer ON the enemy healer. The two can be different players at the same time. See [KORHSIGHT]({{ '/hardkorh/korhsight/' | relative_url }}).

## Working with healer CC

- HARDKORH looks for windows to Incapacitating Roar or Cyclone the enemy healer, respecting diminishing returns and immunities.
- When the HOLD STILL cue appears, stop moving: the Cyclone hard cast is about to start. Moving cancels it.
- HARDKORH never Roars a target your own bleeds would break, and it does not Roar a target that is already controlled.

The full four-stage Cyclone is on the [KORHTRAP]({{ '/hardkorh/korhtrap/' | relative_url }}) page.

## Why your movement matters

Many HARDKORH decisions need a good position before they can fire:

- Cyclone is a hard cast; it waits until you stand still and have line of sight, and moving cancels it.
- Incapacitating Roar needs the target within about 10 yards.
- Arena Burst needs the KILL target within about 8 yards.
- Typhoon needs the target in front of you.

Good positioning creates these opportunities; poor positioning silently removes them.

## Burst windows

With Arena Burst ON, Incarnation or Berserk is used in a kill window. A window needs all of these:

- the enemy healer controlled with enough time left;
- your Rake and Rip up on the KILL;
- you in Cat, close to the KILL and healthy enough;
- the window stable for a moment.

One major cooldown is spent per window. The BURST row shows SETUP or HOLD while a window is building, and GO when it fires. Against a team without a healer, the Arena kill-window logic never spends the major cooldown; burst those games yourself. See [Arena Burst]({{ '/hardkorh/arena-burst/' | relative_url }}).

## Defensive response

When you take pressure, HARDKORH escalates Barkskin, Survival Instincts and Healthstone, and can go into a defensive Bear Form. Your job is to break line of sight and kite while it stabilizes you. See [KORHSHIELD]({{ '/hardkorh/korhshield/' | relative_url }}).

## When not to break control

HARDKORH holds damage on a KILL target that is in Cyclone or breakable CC; the HOLD row shows the reason. Do not force damage on that target manually; switch to another enemy if you want to keep pressure.

## Solo Shuffle notes

- The session controls survive between rounds; they reset only on a reload or relog.
- Mark of the Wild is restored automatically out of combat at round start.
- Re-target your kill target at the start of each round.

## Avoid fighting the system

- Do not spam the same abilities HARDKORH is casting. Duplicate input can collide with its requests.
- If you want full manual control for a moment, press your Enable Plugin key. One press stops everything, another press resumes LIVE.
- Read the HOLD row before assuming something is wrong. A held KILL target in Cyclone or breakable CC is deliberate.
