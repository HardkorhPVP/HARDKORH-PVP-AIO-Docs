<div class="notice--warning" markdown="1">
**PvP first**

HARDKORH is designed for PvP. It contains no dungeon- or raid-specific logic: no boss mechanics, no affix handling, no AoE target logic. It is not a raid or Mythic+ optimized rotation.
</div>

## Outside PvP

- The Cat rotation runs on your current WoW target. Turn **Option C Live Targeting OFF**: ON only attacks enemy players, so it does nothing to NPCs or target dummies.
- Incarnation or Berserk is used on cooldown while you are in Cat and in combat.
- Barkskin, Survival Instincts, Healthstone and Predatory Swiftness Regrowth still work.
- Skull Bash still interrupts your target, and Mark of the Wild is kept up.
- HARDKORH does not shift you into Cat or Bear automatically outside PvP. Enter Cat Form yourself.
- Incapacitating Roar, Cyclone and Arena Burst need enemy players and stay idle.

## A good place to practice

A target dummy is a good place to check your keybinds, HUD placement and the basic rotation before your first PvP session.
