HARDKORH is a Project Sylvanas plugin. Once it is installed, it appears in the Project Sylvanas menu as **HARDKORH PVP AIO** when you log in on a Feral Druid, and as **HARDKORH PVP AIO - BALANCE** on a Balance Druid.

<div class="notice--info" markdown="1">
**Find us in the Project Sylvanas Marketplace**

HARDKORH PVP AIO is distributed through the Project Sylvanas Marketplace. Look for **HARDKORH PVP AIO** there.
</div>

## Confirming the install

Log in on your Feral Druid and open the Project Sylvanas menu. The **HARDKORH PVP AIO** entry opens the HARDKORH settings page. The short build line in its GENERAL OPTIONS card reads **HARDKORH PVP AIO v1.0.0 - Feral M6.6**: v1.0.0 is the product version and M6.6 the Feral engine version.

{% include hk-figure.html src="/assets/images/screenshots/01-hardkorh-settings-overview.png" alt="The HARDKORH PVP AIO v1.0.0 settings page open in the Project Sylvanas menu, with the KORHVISION HUD reading LIVE on the left" w="1574" h="1304" caption="HARDKORH PVP AIO v1.0.0 (Feral M6.6) in the settings interface, with the KORHVISION HUD LIVE." %}

On a Balance Druid, the **HARDKORH PVP AIO - BALANCE** entry opens the Balance settings page instead; see [Balance Settings]({{ '/balance/settings/' | relative_url }}). Each specialization shows only its own page, and changing specialization reloads HARDKORH.

If the entry is missing, check that you are on a Druid in Feral or Balance specialization and that Project Sylvanas is up to date.
