## v1.0.0

Initial Feral Druid PvP release for World of Warcraft: Midnight on Project Sylvanas.

- **KORHSIGHT** sticky KILL targeting: one validated enemy player at a time, and your own target switch always wins.
- **KORHTRAP** healer-focused crowd control: Incapacitating Roar and a four-stage, cue-guided Cyclone, with diminishing-returns and immunity awareness.
- **KORHSHIELD** layered defensives: Barkskin, Survival Instincts, Healthstone, Predatory Swiftness Regrowth and an owned defensive Bear Form with its own Bear toolkit.
- **Arena Burst**: Incarnation or Berserk in healer-controlled kill windows, with its own switch and keybind.
- **KORHKICK** Skull Bash interrupts at the cast progress you choose.
- **KORHVISION** combat HUD with an optional Diagnostics Panel and a Cyclone cue with sound.
- One master switch (Enable Plugin = LIVE), a single settings page, four player keybinds and a safe default-disabled start.
- **KORHDIAG** change-based diagnostic logging for support.
- Complete guide: required talent build, gear and stats for World of Warcraft 12.1.5, Midnight Season 2, settings and PvP playstyle.
