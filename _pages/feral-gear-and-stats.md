HARDKORH does not depend on any specific item. It reads your abilities and cooldowns from the game, and the only item it uses is a Healthstone from your bags. Gear affects how hard you hit and how long you survive, not how HARDKORH decides.

## Recommended PvP stat priority

1. **Agility**
2. **Mastery**
3. **Versatility**
4. **Haste**
5. **Critical Strike**

- **Agility** is your main offensive stat and stays at the top of the list.
- **Mastery** is the secondary stat this setup values most. Feral's Mastery adds to your bleeds and to your finishing moves, which is where this build does its damage.
- **Versatility** matters a great deal in PvP. It raises your damage and also helps you take less and heal more, the durability Feral leans on between bursts.
- **Haste** comes next.
- **Critical Strike** is last in this order.

This order belongs to this HARDKORH build in this season. It is not a rule for every Feral build or for later seasons, and it comes with no stat targets or caps.

## PvP gearing principles

- **Prefer proper PvP gear.** PvP items carry PvP combat scaling, which makes them the stronger choice in Arena, Solo Shuffle and Battlegrounds.
- **Equip two PvP trinkets.** The PvP trinket set benefit only applies when both trinket slots hold PvP trinkets.
- **Lean on Mastery and Versatility where you can.** Not every slot offers both; take them where the item allows.
- **Plan trinkets around your burst.** HARDKORH never uses trinkets for you. Where a trinket rewards timing, line it up with your big offensive windows, above all Incarnation: Avatar of Ashamane.
- **Keep big upgrades.** Do not give up a large PvP item-level upgrade only to tidy your secondary stats unless you have a clear reason.

## Catalyst and class-set (tier) pieces

The Catalyst can turn qualifying PvP items from this season into class-set (tier) items. For this setup, choose what you feed it carefully: a strong PvP piece whose secondary stats already fit the priority above is the best starting point, where practical.

The goal is the full set, both bonuses:

| Set bonus | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **2-piece** | Once Incarnation: Avatar of Ashamane ends, you deal increased damage for a while. How long that bonus lasts depends on the combo points you spent during Incarnation. |
| **4-piece** | Incarnation: Avatar of Ashamane gains 10 extra seconds. |

Both bonuses make each Incarnation window longer and more rewarding. That is why the 4-piece set is part of the recommended plan.

## Trinkets

Run two PvP trinkets. The HardkorhPVP recommended pair is **Galactic Gladiator's Medallion** and **Galactic Gladiator's Insignia of Alacrity**, and the pair is a core part of this competitive PvP plan. HARDKORH never uses trinkets for you; any trinket you activate is your call.

## Embellishments

Wear two embellishments, the most you can equip at once, and make both PvP-appropriate options that support Versatility. HARDKORH's rotation does not rely on any particular embellishment effect, so choose Versatility-oriented embellishments that fit your crafted slots and your current gear plan.
