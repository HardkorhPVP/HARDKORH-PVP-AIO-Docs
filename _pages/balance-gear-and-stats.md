HARDKORH's PvP intelligence does not require perfect equipment. It reads your abilities, costs, cast times and cooldowns from the game, and the only item it uses is a Healthstone from your bags. Gear changes how hard you hit and how long you survive, not how HARDKORH decides. This page shows the recommended competitive PvP gearing direction for Balance Druid.

<div class="notice--info" markdown="1">
**Seasonal recommendation**

This is the HardkorhPVP recommended gearing plan for HARDKORH Balance in World of Warcraft 12.1.5, Midnight Season 2. Items, stats and set bonuses change between patches and seasons, so treat it as advice for this season, not a permanent rule and not a requirement for HARDKORH to work.

## Versatility and Haste

On every crafted or stat-selectable piece, choose **Versatility / Haste**.

- **Versatility** raises your damage and healing and reduces the damage you take: the durability that keeps a caster alive through enemy goes.
- **Haste** shortens your casts and your global cooldown. For Balance that means faster Wrath and Starfire, more actions in every burst window, and a shorter Cyclone cast, so the moments when HARDKORH asks you to stand still are shorter too.

This plan sets the crafted-stat direction only; it comes with no stat targets, caps or breakpoints.

## PvP gearing principles

- **Prefer PvP equipment.** PvP items carry PvP combat scaling, which makes them the stronger choice in Arena, Solo Shuffle and Battlegrounds.
- **Equip two PvP trinkets.** The PvP trinket set benefit only applies when both trinket slots hold PvP trinkets.
- **Versatility / Haste** on every crafted or stat-selectable piece.
- **Aim for the 2-piece tier bonus.** It is the target; the 4-piece is not forced.
- **Two embellishments at most.** That is the most you can equip at once.

## Tier set: target the 2-piece

| Set bonus | What it does | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2-piece** | Starsurge deals more damage, and Starfall gains an extra immediate Astral damage effect. | **The target.** Get it first. |
| **4-piece** | An Eclipse-related damage bonus. | Welcome, but not the default goal. |

The 2-piece strengthens the spender HARDKORH uses throughout its pressure: Starsurge. HARDKORH does not cast Starfall automatically, so the Starfall part of the bonus applies when you cast Starfall yourself. See [Scope & Limits]({{ '/balance/scope-and-limits/' | relative_url }}).

Reach the 2-piece before anything else in your tier slots, and do not give up a stronger PvP piece only to complete the 4-piece. The full set in the gear table below converts four pieces, so a completed plan happens to include the 4-piece as well.

## Trinkets

Run two PvP trinkets. The HardkorhPVP recommended pair is **Galactic Gladiator's Medallion** and **Galactic Gladiator's Insignia of Alacrity**. HARDKORH never uses trinkets for you; any trinket you activate is your call.

## Embellishments

You can wear two embellishments at most. The recommended embellishment for this plan is **Arcanoweave Lining**, which takes one of your two embellishment slots. This plan names no second embellishment, so the second slot is your choice.
